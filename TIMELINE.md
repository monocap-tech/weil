# RH research timeline

**Established:** 2026-11-24. **Status:** living, retrospective, incomplete chronology. No proof of the Riemann hypothesis is claimed.

This timeline connects the public Weil-defect, formalization and arithmetic–spectral comparison investigations. It records identifiable research transitions, not just successful results. Dates below are calendar dates in America/Los_Angeles unless expressly marked UTC. A dated commit establishes that a record existed at that time; it does not establish the original discovery date, initial public availability or independent mathematical correctness. Historical entries are reconstructed from the linked records.

## Maintenance

GitHub holds the public chronology and source evidence; an internal project registry tracks historical reconstruction and review. Update this timeline when an investigation changes formulation, a material obstruction or negative result is identified, a proof obligation changes standing, or a reproducible result is published. Every dated entry should have an event, its mathematical or methodological significance, its scope/standing, and a stable source link; include separate verification/publication dates when established. Preserve superseded milestones and label corrections. Do not publish private source material without review. No completion claim is implied by inclusion in this timeline.
